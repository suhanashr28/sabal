Our Love Story ♡

Happy Birthday, My Love ❤️

If someone had told me a few years ago that the boy I barely knew would one day become the most important person in my life, I probably would have laughed.

Because honestly, our story was never something I expected.

We were just two people in the same class who didn't even know each other properly. I didn't know that you existed, and you probably didn't know much about me either. There was nothing between us—not even a little thought that one day we would become us. And then our friends decided to do what friends do best… they started shipping us. 😂❤️ At that time, I was actually offended. I never understood why they were putting our names together. If someone had told me then, “One day, you're going to fall in love with this boy,” I would have never believed them. But somehow, life had already started writing our story. Then came our senior farewell. Everyone was finding their partner for the dance, and somehow I was left without one. My friend asked you to dance with me, and you said yes. That simple “yes” became one of the most important yeses of my life.

I still remember how nervous and shy I was around you. We didn't talk much while practicing. We were just two awkward people trying to learn a dance together. But then came 24th March 2023. We were backstage, standing in that little corner beside the table. I was nervous, and you noticed. You told me, “Be careful. Don't be nervous.” Maybe for you, those were just two simple sentences. But for me, something changed that day.

I can't explain exactly what happened inside my heart, but I remember the butterflies. I remember the feeling. I remember suddenly becoming so aware of you. Maybe I was already falling for you that day. Maybe my heart knew before I did. After that, I started liking you.

But the funny thing was… you still weren't interested in me. 😂 Our friends tried to make you talk to me, but you didn't. And then one day, you finally talked to me. It was such a small conversation, but I remember how happy I felt afterward. Sometimes it's crazy how the smallest things become the biggest memories when they involve someone you love. Then Class 11 was coming to an end. Some of my friends asked you questions about me, and honestly, your answers hurt me so much. I remember feeling completely broken that day. I thought that was it. I thought whatever little hope I had was gone. I thought, “Maybe this story was never meant to happen.”

But life had another surprise waiting for me. Because the very next day… you confessed to me. And suddenly, the ending I thought I was facing became the beginning of our story.

I don't think either of us knew what we were getting ourselves into. We didn't know how many memories we would make. We didn't know how many times we would laugh until our stomachs hurt. We didn't know how many silly arguments we would have. We didn't know there would be days when we would misunderstand each other, hurt each other, miss each other, or feel like everything was too difficult. We didn't know how much we would grow. We didn't know that three years later, I would still be sitting here writing about you with the same butterflies. Our relationship hasn't been perfect. And honestly, I don't want a perfect love story. I want ours. The messy one. The emotional one. The one with stupid fights and random laughs. The one with apologies, forgiveness, late conversations, missing each other, making memories, and choosing each other again and again. Because that's what makes our story real.

You have seen different versions of me. You've seen me happy. You've seen me angry. You've seen me emotional. You've seen me overthink the smallest things. You've seen my childish side, my stubborn side, my sensitive side, and all the parts of me that I don't show everyone. And somehow, you're still here.

Thank you for loving me through all of it. Thank you for being patient with me. Thank you for listening to my endless talks. Thank you for making me laugh when I don't feel like smiling. Thank you for every little thing you do that you probably don't even realize means so much to me.

And now, three years later, when I look back at everything, I realize how beautiful and unexpected our journey has been. You weren't someone I was looking for. You weren't someone I expected. You weren't someone I imagined falling in love with. But somehow, you became someone I can't imagine my life without. And maybe that's the most beautiful thing about love. Sometimes the person you never expected becomes the person you never want to lose.

Today isn't just another birthday. It's the birthday of the person who became one of the biggest parts of my life. So today, I want you to know how incredibly proud I am of you. I hope you never forget how special you are. I hope you keep chasing your dreams, even when things get difficult. I hope you become everything you've ever wanted to become. I hope life gives you countless reasons to smile. I hope you achieve everything you work so hard for. And whenever life becomes difficult, I hope you remember that there is someone who believes in you, is proud of you, and will always wish the best for you. Me. ❤️

I don't know what the future holds for us. I don't know where life will take us. I don't know how many more chapters our story will have. But I know one thing. If I could go back to the beginning and choose again… I would still choose that dance. I would still choose that nervous little moment backstage. I would still choose the butterflies. I would still choose you. Because somehow, out of all the people in this world, our paths crossed. Two strangers became friends. Friends became something more. And that something became a love story that I will always keep close to my heart.

So, on your birthday, I just want to say: Happy Birthday to the boy who was once a stranger to me, but somehow became my favorite person. ❤️ Happy Birthday to the person who makes ordinary days feel special. Happy Birthday to my partner, my favorite human, my safe place, my happiness, and the person with whom I've shared some of the most beautiful moments of my life. I hope this new year of your life brings you everything your heart desires. I hope you grow, succeed, travel, laugh, dream bigger, and become even more amazing than you already are. And I hope, somehow, I get to be there for many of those moments. Because if our first three years taught me anything, it's that the most beautiful things can come from the most unexpected beginnings. Three years ago, I didn't even know you existed. Today, I can't imagine my story without you.

Happy Birthday, my love. ❤️ Thank you for becoming my unexpected love story. Thank you for being you. And thank you for choosing me. I love you more than these words could ever explain. Here's to you. Here's to us. Here's to the memories we've already made. And here's to all the memories we haven't created yet.

Happy Birthday, my love. My favorite unexpected beginning. My favorite person. My forever "don't be nervous." ❤️

I love you. Always. 🫶🏻❤️
