A LITTLE NOTE FOR YOU

The day I will never forget

Our first date was so special to me. It was my first time going on a date like that, and before I saw you I was feeling so many things at once. I was nervous — the kind of nervous that makes your heart beat a little faster and makes you wonder if you will say the right things. But underneath all of that, I was also so happy, because I knew I was going to spend the day with you.

When we were together, those nerves slowly started to feel softer. We walked, talked, and went everywhere together, and somehow even the smallest moments felt important. There was nothing perfect or planned about every second, but that is exactly why it felt so real. It was just us, sharing time, making little memories, and letting the day unfold.

I still think about the smiles, the conversations, and the quiet moments in between. I remember how happy I felt just being beside you. You made my nervous heart feel comfortable, and you made that day feel warm, safe, and beautiful.

This one picture is all I have from our first date, but it holds so much for me. It reminds me of where our story began — of the first butterflies, the first walk together, and the happiness of realizing that this moment with you was something I wanted to remember forever.

Thank you for making my first date such a beautiful memory. No matter how much time passes, I will always look back at this day with a full heart. It was the beginning of so many lovely moments with you, and I feel so lucky that it was you. ♡

“One photo, a thousand feelings.”
