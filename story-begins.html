A LITTLE NOTE FOR YOU

The moment my feelings became real

Our story began on that Alice reception farewell day. At first, it may have looked like just another day — people around us, beautiful clothes, laughter, and a room full of memories being made. But for me, something quietly changed that day. Somewhere between all those moments, I started to realize that what I felt for you was becoming more than just a simple feeling.

I remember seeing you and feeling a happiness that was different. I did not know how to explain it perfectly then, but my heart felt softer whenever you were near. In the middle of the crowd, you were the person my eyes wanted to find. In all the noise and movement of the day, being close to you felt like my favorite part.

That farewell day became the beginning of something so precious to me. It was the day I began to understand that I had feelings for you, and the day I slowly started falling in love with you. Maybe love does not always arrive with a big announcement. Sometimes it begins in a small smile, a shared moment, or the way one person makes an ordinary day feel unforgettable.

These pictures hold that feeling for me. They remind me of us standing together, surrounded by everyone, yet creating a memory that feels completely ours. I look at them and remember the warmth, the excitement, and the little butterflies I did not yet know how to name.

Thank you for being the beginning of this beautiful chapter in my life. From that day until now, every memory with you has made my heart more certain. Our story began there, and I will always be grateful that it began with you. ♡
